Third level: Form 7(or any number) letter words that ends with "a"(or any letter)
Fourth level: Form 7(or any number) letter words that starts with "a"(or any letter), and also contains the letter "d"(or any letter)
Fifth level: Form 7(or any number) letter words that ends with "a"(or any letter), and also contains the letter "d"(or any letter)
