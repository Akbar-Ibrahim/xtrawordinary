1. Beginning and End: 
We provide a word to the player, they have to use the last letter of our word to form a word. Then, we use the last letter of their word to form a word, and they use the last letter of our word to form a word. And on and on. 
This game has two variations:
First variation:
First level: Player has to use the last letter of our word to form a word.
Second level: Everything with the previous level, except that the word they enter must be of the same length as the word we provide them.

Second variation:
First level: They need to use the last two letters of the word we provide them to for their word. For example, if we give them the word "place", their word must start with "ce".
Second level: Everything with the previous level, except that the word they enter must be of the same length as the word we provide them.

Note: Each level requires player to enter 100 words.

When a player enters a word, the following should happen:

So, here, for example, for the first level, whatever word they enter must begin with the last letter of the word we provide. And for the second level, whatever word they enter must begin with the last two letters of the word we provide.
- When they enter a correct word, we make an API call to the back-end and check our sample array if the word they entered is present in it. If it isn't we tell them that's not a valid word. If it's present in the array, edit field is cleared for them to enter the next word.
- Player must not be allowed to enter a word they've already entered before. So, if they do that, we tell them they already entered that word.
- What time frame should we allow for them to enter a word? The way I want this is to work is, for every time they have to enter a word, there should be a count down. If they don't enter the word in that window, they lose. So, the time frame should be such that it keeps the players on their toes.
