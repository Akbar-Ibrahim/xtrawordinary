NEXT STEPS — xtraWordinary

DUEL SYSTEM DEPTH
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Duel Spectator Mode
   Players set a duel as Public or Private when issuing the challenge.
   Spectators join the same real-time room in read-only mode and see the
   live word feed, timer, and lives. Spectator emoji reactions (👀 🔥 😬)
   flash briefly on screen for both players. A spectator count is shown
   to both players (e.g. "👁 4 watching"). A "Live Now" section on the
   Duels lobby shows ongoing public duels that friends are in.
   Builds on the existing WebSocket / DuelRoomRegistry infrastructure.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GROUP VS GROUP CHALLENGES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2. Huddle Mode (Phase 1)
   Two groups challenge each other to a synchronous game session.
   One designated typist per group; the rest shout answers. Both groups
   start at the same time via the existing mutual-Ready + countdown flow.
   Same puzzle/seed given to both sides. Works for ALL duel games.
   Entry point: "Challenge Another Group" on the Group detail page.
   No new game logic needed — same engine as 1v1 duels.

3. Team Race Mode (Phase 2)
   Every group member joins on their own device. Members play
   simultaneously; answers are pooled and de-duplicated for the team.
   Real-time team score feed shows both groups' aggregate tallies live.
   Works for simultaneous race games: Anagram Solver, No Repeats, Word
   Stack, Letter Pool, Word Maker, Word Split, Word Scramble.
   Design questions to resolve: player cap, score normalisation for
   unequal group sizes, quorum (min members needed to start).

4. Relay Mode (Phase 3)
   Teams rotate which member is the active typist each round — each
   member gets a turn. Works for turn-based games.
   Build after Huddle and Team Race are stable.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WORD WARS — RECURRING COMPETITIVE EVENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5. Solo Wars (individual bracket tournament)
   A recurring bracket-style tournament open to all players.
   Players sign up during a registration window; the bracket is seeded
   by ELO rating. Each round is a best-of-3 series (different game types
   per round, chosen by the platform). Players have a 24-hour window to
   complete each battle. Losers are eliminated; the winner of the final
   earns a permanent "Word Wars Champion — Week N" badge on their profile.
   A hall of fame page records all past champions. Reuses the existing
   duel engine, ELO system, and badge/achievement infrastructure.

6. Guild Wars (group vs group tournament)
   Group admins declare war on another group. Each group fields its members
   across a set of assigned games played within a fixed window (e.g. 48 hrs).
   Scores are pooled per group; the group with the higher combined total wins.
   Winning group earns a victory badge on their group page and a war history
   log. Unequal group sizes are handled by score normalisation. Builds on
   the existing groups and group-rounds infrastructure.

   Both formats live under the same "Word Wars" nav entry. Shared components:
   registration/scheduling layer, bracket/scoreboard UI, result tracking,
   and war history pages. Gameplay itself reuses duels and group rounds.
