NEXT STEPS — xtraWordinary

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DUEL SYSTEM DEPTH
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Duel Tournaments
   A bracket-style mini-tournament where 4–8 players sign up, get seeded
   by ELO, and play single-elimination rounds. The platform already has
   rooms, ELO, and lobby infrastructure; this would be the crown jewel
   of the duel system.

2. Duel Achievements
   The platform has 19 achievement definitions but none are duel-specific.
   Milestones like "Win 10 duels", "Achieve 1400 ELO", "Win 5 races in a
   row" would motivate competitive play.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DAILY & STREAKS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3. Daily Duel
   A single shared puzzle every day where everyone's matched against the
   same seed, building a daily all-time leaderboard. Fits naturally
   alongside the existing daily challenge.

4. Streak Visualization on the Stats Page
   The streak system exists in the backend but the stats page could show
   a heatmap calendar (like GitHub contributions) to make streaks feel
   more satisfying.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DISCOVERY & ONBOARDING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5. Game Search and Filtering on the Home Page
   With 23 games, new users may feel overwhelmed. A search bar, difficulty
   filter, and "popular this week" sort would help them find games they
   enjoy faster.

6. Improved Onboarding Flow
   New users currently land on the home grid with no guidance. A short
   "pick your first game" wizard or a welcome banner for new accounts
   would reduce drop-off.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SOCIAL & COMMUNITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7. In-App Notifications Panel
   Friend requests, group invites, challenge results, and comment replies
   currently require the user to navigate to the right page. A
   notification bell in the nav with a dropdown would surface these
   without disruption.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRIORITY ORDER (highest user impact first)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Duel Achievements   — makes recent duel investment pay off in engagement
  2. In-App Notifications Panel — ties all social features together
  3. Game Search / Filtering — helps new users find games faster
