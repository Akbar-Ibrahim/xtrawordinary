## Guild Wars — Notifications & Alerts
#332 - Alert group admins when a match is ready to play
       (When the bracket is drawn and each round's matches are created, send an in-app
        notification to the admin/owner of each participating group so they know it's their
        turn to start a match. Notification type: guild_war_match_ready. Include match ID,
        opponent group name, round number, and a link to the bracket page.
        Both MemStorage and MySQLStorage must be updated.)

## Guild Wars — Group Stats
#333 - Add Guild Wars group stats to the group leaderboard tab
       (Add a Guild Wars section to the group detail leaderboard/stats tab showing: total
        tournaments entered, championships won, current active tournament status, and a list
        of past tournament results with bracket links. Pull from GET /api/groups/:id/guild-wars
        and enrich as needed. Both MemStorage and MySQLStorage must be updated.)

## Duel Discovery
#335 - Show a "players waiting" badge on game cards when duels are open
       (When open duel challenges exist for a duel-eligible game, show a small badge on that
        game's card in the main games browser — e.g. "2 waiting" — so players can spot active
        games before visiting each detail page. Poll /api/duels/open on a ~60s interval from
        the games list. Only show the badge for authenticated users.)
