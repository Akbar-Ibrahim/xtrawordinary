xtraWordinary — Monetization Guide
=====================================

GUIDING PRINCIPLE
-----------------
Free should be genuinely fun. Paid should be obviously better.
Free users get just enough to get hooked. Paid users get everything.
The daily challenge is always free — it's the core habit loop and must never be gated.


FREE vs PAID TIER SPLIT
-----------------------

-------------------------------|-----------------------------|---------------------------

FEATURE BREAKDOWN
-----------------

1. PLAY LIMITS (core gate)
   - Free users get ~10-15 plays per day across all games
   - Paid users get unlimited plays
   - Survival modes are the best candidates for limits — they're addictive and replayable
   - Don't fully block modes — give free users a taste (e.g. 3 Survival plays/day)
     so they know what they're missing before hitting the gate

2. LEADERBOARD
   - Everyone can VIEW the leaderboard (free users need this for FOMO)
   - Only paid users APPEAR on it
   - Free users see the board, feel the pull, pay to get their name up there

3. GROUPS
   - Joining a group = always free (keeps communities active and valuable)
   - Creating a group = paid feature
   - Free users capped at 1 group membership; paid users in unlimited groups
   - Group creators are the ones willing to invest — they're the right target for the gate

4. ADVANCED STATS
   - Free: basic totals (games played, wins, current streak)
   - Paid: per-game breakdowns, score trends over time, word history,
     best/worst sessions, full achievement progress
   - All data is already tracked — this is mostly a display gate

5. STREAK SHIELDS
   - We already have a streak system with real emotional attachment
   - Free users lose their streak if they miss a day
   - Paid users get shields (e.g. 2/month) to protect against a missed day
   - Reference: Duolingo makes enormous revenue from this mechanic alone

6. ADS
   - Free users see banner/interstitial ads between games
   - Paid users get an ad-free experience
   - Low friction upsell — many users will pay just to remove ads
   - Doesn't require building new gameplay features

7. HINTS
   - Free users get a small daily hint budget (e.g. 5 total across all games)
   - Paid users get generous or unlimited hints
   - Soft gate — doesn't block play but makes paid feel noticeably better

8. PROFILE & CUSTOMIZATION
   - Paid users unlock: "Pro" badge, special titles, custom color themes, avatar frames
   - Doubles as social signalling — Pro badges on the leaderboard create aspiration
   - Low-effort to build, high perceived value

9. WORD HISTORY / GAME REPLAY
   - Paid users can review any past game: words found, words missed, score breakdown
   - Free users only see their current session
   - Appeals to the "I want to improve" player

10. ZEN MODE
    - Paid-only mode that removes time pressure from Survival games
    - Same game mechanics, no countdown
    - Appeals to players who find timers stressful but still want to play

11. EARLY GAME ACCESS
    - New games go to paid users first
    - Free users get them one week later
    - Low-effort to implement (a flag on the game record)
    - Creates real incentive to subscribe ahead of new releases

12. FRIEND CHALLENGES
    - Free users: limited challenges per day (e.g. 3 send/receive)
    - Paid users: unlimited
    - Challenges are a viral mechanic — don't kill them entirely for free users


NOTES & CAUTIONS
----------------
- Never hard-wall the daily challenge — it's the #1 retention driver
- Don't make free so restrictive that users uninstall before getting hooked
  The goal is: free users play enough to love the app, then hit a meaningful limit
- The leaderboard should never be fully hidden from free users —
  social proof and FOMO are more powerful than a hard gate
- "Taste then gate" beats "cold wall" — let users experience premium features
  briefly before locking them, so they know exactly what they're paying for


NAMED PLAN TIERS (CONSUMER)
----------------------------

Free
- All games (subject to daily play limits)
- Guest stats in local storage
- Global leaderboard viewing
- Daily challenge (always)

Pro (~$4-6/month)
- Unlimited plays
- Stats synced to account permanently
- Full achievement history & extended streak tracking
- Appear on global leaderboard (with Pro badge)
- Streak shields (2/month)
- Ad-free
- Full profile customization

Challenger (~$8-10/month)
- Everything in Pro
- Unlimited friend challenges
- Create unlimited private groups
- Group round history
- Early access to new games
- Zen mode (no timers on Survival games)


SCHOOL / TEACHER PLANS
-----------------------

Teacher Plan (~$10-15/month per teacher)
- Create a "classroom" (private group) with a student join code
- Assign specific games as homework (group rounds with a deadline)
- Per-student performance dashboard
- Track vocabulary progress over time
- Lock game settings (e.g. force Guided mode, set difficulty)

School License (~$500-2,000/year per school)
- Covers all teachers and students at the school
- School-level admin dashboard
- Roster management (CSV import or Google Classroom)
- Progress reports exportable for parents/admin
- Custom branding (school name/logo)

Why schools are a strong opportunity:
- Platform already has groups, rounds, seeded puzzles, and leaderboards
- One teacher brings an entire class (high acquisition leverage)
- Students who enjoy it can convert to paying individual users later
- closesAt column is already in the group_rounds schema (deadline UI just needs building)
- Vocabulary improvement is a clear, defensible educational outcome

Infrastructure already in place for classrooms:

Feature                    | Already Built | Needs Addition
---------------------------|---------------|--------------------------------

Seeded/shared puzzles      | YES           | Homework deadline setting
Leaderboard per group      | YES           | Per-student progress view

24 vocabulary games        | YES           | -
